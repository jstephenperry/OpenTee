About the data GitHub

Golf scorecards, exactly as the course publishes them.

Course websites bury scorecard data in PDFs and booking widgets, and it disappears when sites are redesigned. OpenTee keeps it in the open — yardages, pars, stroke indexes and ratings per tee. Find your course, pick the tees you play, print a card.
