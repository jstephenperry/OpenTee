About the data

OpenTee is an open database of golf course scorecard data — yardages, pars, stroke indexes and ratings per tee, as published by courses. This site is a static view of that database: every page here is generated from the same PostgreSQL schema the project uses, so what you read is what the database holds.

What is in it today

Public courses across the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex: municipal, daily-fee, resort and public-access semi-private facilities. Private clubs are deliberately out of scope, and the discovery pass recorded what it rejected and why, so stale directory listings for closed or private courses cannot creep back in.

How a number gets here

  • Discovery enumerated every public course in the metro area, keeping a record of rejections.
  • Collection transcribed published scorecards under a standing rule: never invent a number.
  • Normalization and generation validate every course against the schema's own constraints plus golf-domain sanity, and refuse to emit anything inconsistent.
  • Verification adversarially re-checked a sample against the published cards, trying to refute rather than confirm. Its corrections are stored with their evidence, applied on top of the untouched raw harvest.

Every course page lists the sources behind its data. Nothing here has been checked against an official printed card in hand, so treat it as community data awaiting verification.

Missing numbers stay missing

A tee whose per-hole card is not published is stored with its total only, and shows on this site as incomplete — it cannot be added to a printed card. An unrated course has no rating, not a plausible-looking one. Par-3 and executive courses without ratings are first-class citizens here.

Where a course's own printed total disagrees with the sum of its holes, the card says so rather than quietly picking a winner. That is usually a transcription error, and occasionally a genuine misprint on the course's card.

Men's, women's and unisex rows

Real cards print par and stroke index per gender, and the same physical tee is often rated separately for men and women. The database stores one set of yardages with as many rating rows as the course publishes, so a card here shows every row the course prints — no duplicated tees, no averaged ratings.

Corrections welcome

If a card here disagrees with the one in the pro shop, the card in the pro shop wins. Open an issue on GitHub with the course and what it should say — ideally with a photo of the printed card.
